# Darcy Visits the Parsonage

## Story beat

Darcy repeatedly calls at Hunsford but communicates his interest so poorly that Charlotte reads him more accurately than Elizabeth does.

## Why it matters

Proximity intensifies attraction without resolving the interpretive gap between them.

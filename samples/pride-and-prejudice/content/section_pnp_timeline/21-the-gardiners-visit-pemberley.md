# The Gardiners Visit Pemberley

## Story beat

Elizabeth agrees to tour Pemberley only after hearing that Darcy is absent; the estate and housekeeper’s testimony challenge her former picture of him.

## Why it matters

Place becomes character evidence before Darcy has any opportunity to argue for himself.

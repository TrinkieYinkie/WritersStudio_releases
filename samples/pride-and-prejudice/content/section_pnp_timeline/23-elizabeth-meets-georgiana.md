# Elizabeth Meets Georgiana

## Story beat

Darcy introduces Elizabeth to Georgiana; Caroline attempts to revive old embarrassment, but Darcy’s admiration remains visible.

## Why it matters

Elizabeth sees Darcy as a brother and host, while his social circle recognizes her new importance.

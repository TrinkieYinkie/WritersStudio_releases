# Mrs Gardiner Reveals Darcy’s Role

## Story beat

Mrs Gardiner explains that Darcy found the couple, paid Wickham’s debts, arranged the commission, and insisted on secrecy.

## Why it matters

Elizabeth gains the clearest evidence yet that Darcy’s principles now operate through generous action.

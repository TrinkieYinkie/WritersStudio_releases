# Wickham Tells His Story

## Story beat

Wickham’s charm and account of Darcy’s supposed injustice confirm what Elizabeth already wants to believe.

## Why it matters

Partial truth becomes emotionally persuasive because it flatters Elizabeth’s existing judgment.

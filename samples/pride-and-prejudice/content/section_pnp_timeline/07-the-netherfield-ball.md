# The Netherfield Ball

## Story beat

Elizabeth dances with Darcy, cannot question absent Wickham, and watches her family expose itself under public scrutiny.

## Why it matters

Attraction, embarrassment, and class anxiety converge in the novel’s first major social set piece.

# Lady Catherine Confronts Elizabeth

## Story beat

Lady Catherine arrives at Longbourn to demand that Elizabeth promise never to marry Darcy; Elizabeth refuses every claim over her choice.

## Why it matters

External opposition forces Elizabeth to acknowledge that she would now accept Darcy if he asked again.

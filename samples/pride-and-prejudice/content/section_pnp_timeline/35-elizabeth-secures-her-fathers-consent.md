# Elizabeth Secures Her Father’s Consent

## Story beat

Mr Bennet initially cannot believe Elizabeth loves Darcy, then gives consent after she explains her respect and confidence in him.

## Why it matters

The approval matters because Elizabeth must distinguish her choice from financial temptation and family astonishment.

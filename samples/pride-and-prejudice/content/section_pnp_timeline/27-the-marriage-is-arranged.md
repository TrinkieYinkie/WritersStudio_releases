# The Marriage Is Arranged

## Story beat

Mr Gardiner reports that Lydia and Wickham will marry if Mr Bennet accepts manageable financial terms.

## Why it matters

The immediate scandal is contained, though the terms conceal Darcy’s decisive contribution.

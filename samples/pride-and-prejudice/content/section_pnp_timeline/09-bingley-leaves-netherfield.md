# Bingley Leaves Netherfield

## Story beat

Bingley’s party departs for London, and Caroline’s letter encourages Jane to abandon hope of his return.

## Why it matters

Outside persuasion succeeds because Bingley is modest and Jane’s reserve is easy to misread.

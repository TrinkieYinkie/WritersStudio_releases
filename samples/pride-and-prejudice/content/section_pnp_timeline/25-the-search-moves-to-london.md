# The Search Moves to London

## Story beat

The Gardiners bring Elizabeth home; Mr Bennet joins the search, and the family waits through uncertainty and public speculation.

## Why it matters

Private negligence becomes collective danger, exposing how reputation constrains every Bennet sister.

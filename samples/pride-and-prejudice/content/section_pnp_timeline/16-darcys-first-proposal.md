# Darcy’s First Proposal

## Story beat

Darcy declares his love while emphasizing the inferiority of Elizabeth’s connections; she refuses and accuses him of arrogance and cruelty.

## Why it matters

Both characters speak truths distorted by pride, forcing the central conflict into the open.

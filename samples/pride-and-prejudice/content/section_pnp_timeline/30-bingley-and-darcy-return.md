# Bingley and Darcy Return

## Story beat

The two friends return to Netherfield and visit Longbourn. Bingley’s attention to Jane revives openly, while Darcy remains guarded.

## Why it matters

Both courtships reopen, but Elizabeth now understands how much cannot be assumed from manner alone.

# Darcy’s Second Proposal

## Story beat

Walking together, Darcy renews his proposal only on the condition that Elizabeth’s feelings have changed; she tells him they have.

## Why it matters

Mutual consent replaces presumption, and each can describe how the other prompted necessary growth.

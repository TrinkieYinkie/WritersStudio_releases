# Jane Goes to London

## Story beat

Jane stays with the Gardiners, hopes to see Caroline, and receives enough coldness to recognize that the friendship was not sincere.

## Why it matters

The visit prolongs separation while giving Jane a clearer account of Caroline’s motives.

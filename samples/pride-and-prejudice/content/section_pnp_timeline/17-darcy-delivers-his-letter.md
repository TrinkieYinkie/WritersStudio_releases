# Darcy Delivers His Letter

## Story beat

Darcy explains his judgment of Jane’s feelings and gives a detailed history of Wickham, including the attempted elopement with Georgiana.

## Why it matters

Evidence replaces accusation and gives Elizabeth the means to test her own interpretation.

# Elizabeth Re-reads the Past

## Story beat

Elizabeth rereads the letter, checks it against memory, and recognizes how vanity made Wickham credible and Darcy hateful.

## Why it matters

The protagonist’s deepest reversal is an act of self-knowledge rather than romantic surrender.

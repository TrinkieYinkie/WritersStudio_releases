# Darcy Learns He May Hope

## Story beat

Lady Catherine reports Elizabeth’s defiance to Darcy, expecting outrage; he instead hears that Elizabeth’s feelings may have changed.

## Why it matters

The antagonist’s attempt to prevent the marriage becomes the information that makes a second proposal possible.

# Meryton Assembly — Scene Pass

> Demo draft: a modern scene exercise created for this WritersStudio sample. It is not text by Jane Austen.

## Goal

Stage Elizabeth and Darcy’s first collision around what each of them notices—and fails to notice.

## Draft

The room rewarded confidence. Bingley crossed it as if every introduction were a gift; Darcy stood as if each one were an examination.

Elizabeth saw the difference before she knew either man. One made strangers feel chosen. The other made them aware of being assessed.

When the music began again, Darcy’s refusal travelled farther than he intended. Elizabeth caught only a few words, but the shape of the insult was unmistakable. She turned it into a story before it could become a wound, and by the next dance she had made Charlotte laugh.

Across the room, Darcy noticed the laughter and misunderstood that too.

## Revision questions

- Keep the point of view with Elizabeth throughout, or allow the final Darcy beat?
- Can the overheard refusal be sharper without quoting the published scene?
- Does Elizabeth’s humour read as resilience, pride, or both?

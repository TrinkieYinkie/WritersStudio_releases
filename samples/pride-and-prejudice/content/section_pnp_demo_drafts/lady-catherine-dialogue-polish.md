# Lady Catherine at Longbourn — Dialogue Polish

> Demo draft: a modern dialogue exercise created for this WritersStudio sample. It is not text by Jane Austen.

## Goal

Build the confrontation through competing definitions of duty, rank, and personal freedom.

## Draft

Lady Catherine treated the rumour as an offence that must confess itself.

“You will deny it.”

Elizabeth heard the command concealed inside the future tense. “I will answer only for my own decisions.”

Every appeal to family honour made the boundary clearer. Lady Catherine believed Elizabeth’s place in the world had been assigned; Elizabeth believed conduct mattered more than assignment. Neither woman mistook the other.

When the carriage finally left, the gravel seemed unusually loud. Elizabeth had promised nothing—and, in refusing to promise, had admitted more to herself than she intended.

## Revision questions

- Does each exchange escalate the underlying argument?
- Can Lady Catherine remain formidable without becoming a caricature?
- Is Elizabeth’s final self-recognition clear enough to launch the resolution?

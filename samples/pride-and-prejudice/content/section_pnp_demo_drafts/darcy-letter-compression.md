# Darcy’s Letter — Compression Exercise

> Demo draft: a modern structural exercise created for this WritersStudio sample. It is not text by Jane Austen.

## Goal

Reduce the letter scene to the sequence of emotional reversals it creates for Elizabeth.

## Beat draft

1. **Resistance:** She begins reading in order to confirm the judgment she has already made.
2. **Irritation:** Darcy’s account of Jane and Bingley sounds proud, but not careless.
3. **Disruption:** Details about Wickham do not fit the story Elizabeth preferred.
4. **Verification:** Memory supplies evidence that vanity had dismissed.
5. **Recognition:** The letter becomes less a defence of Darcy than an indictment of her confidence.

Elizabeth lowers the pages. Nothing outside the letter has changed, yet the moral geometry of every recent scene has shifted.

## Revision questions

- Which beat needs the most page time?
- Should the scene end on shame, curiosity, or the first impulse toward fairness?
- What earlier details should be planted so the reversal feels earned on reread?

# Opening — Exploratory Retelling

> Demo draft: a modern editorial exercise created for this WritersStudio sample. It is not text by Jane Austen.

## Goal

Test whether the opening can introduce the Bennets’ comic family pressure before naming Netherfield’s new tenant.

## Draft

By breakfast, Mrs Bennet had married off an unknown man three different ways.

She knew only that Netherfield Park was occupied again, that its tenant was young, and that someone had estimated his income with gratifying precision. To her, these were not rumours but the first three clauses of a wedding announcement. Mr Bennet listened from behind his book, offering just enough silence to encourage her.

“You must call on him,” she said.

“Must I?”

His mildness made the question dangerous. Around them, five daughters were being moved like pieces on a board none of them had agreed to play.

## Revision questions

- Does the first sentence establish comic pressure quickly enough?
- Is Mr Bennet’s amusement visible without explaining the joke?
- Should Elizabeth appear in the room, or remain an expectation for the next scene?

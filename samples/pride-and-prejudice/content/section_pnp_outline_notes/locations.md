# Locations

| Location | Narrative function |
| --- | --- |
| Longbourn | Bennet family home; affection, embarrassment, and the pressure of the entail coexist. |
| Netherfield Park | Bingley’s rented estate; the first arena for both central courtships. |
| Meryton | Local social centre where the militia and Wickham enter the story. |
| Lucas Lodge | Charlotte’s family home and a practical counterpoint to Longbourn. |
| London | A place of concealment and discovery: Jane’s disappointed visit, Lydia’s search, and Darcy’s intervention. |
| Hunsford Parsonage | Charlotte’s negotiated domestic space and the setting for Darcy’s first proposal. |
| Rosings Park | Lady Catherine’s estate; rank performed as control. |
| Pemberley | Darcy’s Derbyshire estate; stewardship and private character become visible. |
| Lambton | Mrs Gardiner’s former home and the base for the Pemberley visit. |
| Brighton | Offstage site of Lydia’s elopement and the consequence of unchecked impulse. |

Distances and calendar dates are intentionally kept approximate in this sample; the timeline tracks narrative order rather than claiming a definitive scholarly chronology.

# Volume I Outline

## Chapters 1–6 — New arrivals and first impressions

Netherfield is let to Charles Bingley. At the Meryton assembly, Jane and Bingley connect easily while Darcy’s reserve wounds Elizabeth’s pride. Conversation at Lucas Lodge establishes Elizabeth’s amused resistance and Darcy’s growing attention.

## Chapters 7–12 — Netherfield proximity

Jane’s illness brings Elizabeth to Netherfield. Several days of conversation let Darcy admire Elizabeth’s intelligence while Caroline Bingley tries to redirect his attention and protect her own ambitions.

## Chapters 13–18 — Collins, Wickham, and the ball

Mr Collins arrives with marriage and reconciliation in mind. Wickham offers Elizabeth a persuasive account of Darcy’s injustice. The Netherfield ball then concentrates nearly every misunderstanding in public.

## Chapters 19–23 — Proposals and departures

Elizabeth rejects Collins. Charlotte accepts him for practical security. Bingley leaves Netherfield, and Jane must face the possibility that his attachment was weaker than it seemed.

## Volume turn

The first volume closes with two relationships redirected by external judgment: Charlotte chooses Collins; Darcy and his circle separate Bingley from Jane.

# Relationship Map

## Bennet family

Mr and Mrs Bennet have five daughters: Jane, Elizabeth, Mary, Catherine, and Lydia. The Longbourn estate is entailed away from the daughters to Mr Collins, making marriage and financial security persistent family concerns.

## Central pair

**Elizabeth Bennet ↔ Fitzwilliam Darcy:** mutual attention filtered through wounded pride, incomplete evidence, moral challenge, and changed behaviour.

## Parallel courtships

- **Jane Bennet ↔ Charles Bingley:** genuine affection endangered by reserve and outside persuasion.
- **Charlotte Lucas ↔ William Collins:** a pragmatic marriage built around security and careful household management.
- **Lydia Bennet ↔ George Wickham:** impulsive attraction converted into marriage only by outside intervention.

## Pressure and mediation

- Caroline Bingley discourages Bingley’s connection to Jane and hopes to attract Darcy.
- Lady Catherine expects Darcy to marry her daughter and tries to control Elizabeth.
- Mr and Mrs Gardiner offer Elizabeth judgment, affection, travel, and practical help.
- Georgiana Darcy’s warmth helps Elizabeth see Darcy within a loving family relationship.

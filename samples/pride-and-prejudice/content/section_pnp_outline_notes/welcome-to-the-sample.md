# Welcome to the Sample

This project demonstrates how a finished novel can live beside working material in WritersStudio.

## What is authentic source text?

The **Final Manuscript** section contains Jane Austen’s public-domain novel, divided into 61 chapter documents and grouped into three volumes.

## What is sample material?

**Demo Drafts**, **Outline and Notes**, character profiles, timeline summaries, tags, and relationships were created in 2026 specifically for this demo. They are editorial aids, not writings by Jane Austen and not claims about her drafting process.

## Suggested tour

1. Expand the three manuscript volumes.
2. Compare a demo draft with its related published chapter.
3. Open Elizabeth Bennet or Fitzwilliam Darcy in Characters.
4. Filter the Story Timeline by **Elizabeth & Darcy**.
5. Follow a related-document link from an event to the manuscript.

# Text sources and provenance

## Original work

_Pride and Prejudice_ was written by Jane Austen and first published in 1813.
The original English novel is in the public domain.

## Imported transcription

The chapter text in this sample is derived from the Standard Ebooks edition:

- Project: [`standardebooks/jane-austen_pride-and-prejudice`](https://github.com/standardebooks/jane-austen_pride-and-prejudice)
- Pinned revision: `68387166882cfcf9090cc30e44083e094ede782f`
- Source archive SHA-256: `8dd0f6403a4556d0f0c5a21367393d73d4b120f84096a9267d6411cbefc1c7a9`
- Retrieved: 2026-08-19
- Upstream license: [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Standard Ebooks states that the source text is believed to be in the United
States public domain and dedicates its contributors' editorial and formatting
work to the worldwide public domain through CC0. Users outside the United
States remain responsible for checking the law applicable in their
jurisdiction.

The pinned Standard Ebooks production notes state that this edition modernizes
some period-dashes and ungrammatical commas for smoother modern reading. The
sample therefore identifies the exact transcription revision instead of
claiming to reproduce the punctuation of a particular 1813 printing.

The Standard Ebooks transcription is itself based in part on [Project
Gutenberg ebook #1342](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342) and public scans.

## Import boundary

Only the 61 prose chapters are imported into the Final Manuscript section.
Modern descriptions, production notes, navigation, cover art, illustrations,
publisher marks, and website material are not included.

The Demo Drafts, outlines, notes, character summaries, relationship metadata,
and timeline were newly prepared as WritersStudio demonstration material. They
must not be represented as historical Jane Austen manuscripts or scholarship.
